How to Solve Chess Puzzles Online

Chess Puzzles presents tactical positions from real games played on Lichess. Each puzzle shows a board position where one side has a winning move or combination. Your goal is to find the correct sequence of moves. Puzzles range from simple one-move tactics to complex multi-move combinations involving forks, pins, skewers, and checkmates.

Game Features

Choose from four difficulty levels: beginner, intermediate, advanced, and master. The collection includes over 20,000 unique puzzles sourced from real chess games, so there is always a fresh challenge. Your solve rate and statistics are saved locally. The interactive board lets you drag pieces or click to move, and works on both desktop and mobile devices.
